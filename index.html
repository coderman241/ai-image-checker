Reasons

    How to read this result

    • Likely AI-generated — strong clues point that way, such as a generator name in the file data. It is still a hint, not proof.
    • Uncertain — the clues are mixed, weak, or missing. Many real photos and many AI images land here.
    • Likely real / camera photo — camera clues point that way. A fake camera tag can still fool this check.
    • The score is a lean, not a percentage. 55 does not mean “55% AI.” It means the clues lean only slightly toward AI, from a starting point of 50.

    What this does — and doesn't — do

    • It looks at file metadata (camera names, generator keywords, software tags) and a few lightweight image clues (smoothness, saturation).
    • Metadata can be stripped, edited, or faked, so its absence or presence is weak evidence on its own.
    • Many real photos have no camera data after being shared or re-saved, and many AI images can carry fake camera data.
    • Weak clues alone will not reach “likely AI-generated” — they land in “uncertain” instead.
    • There is also a Python version in this repo: ai_image_check.py.

    Heuristic checker only. Treat the verdict as a hint, not proof.
